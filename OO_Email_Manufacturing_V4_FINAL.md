# WHAT CHANGED FROM V3 AND WHY

## The problem V3 had

The Alumni campaign ran for 10 days with zero replies. Stephanie's feedback was direct: "It's still not clear who Outlook Orange is and what they really do. The call to action is not clear either." The V3 manufacturing copy had the same weakness — it described the pain but never explained what Outlook Orange actually does about it, and every CTA was a soft rhetorical question that asked for nothing specific.

## What the Alumni fix taught us

Antoine's rewrite of the Alumni sequence added four things that were missing:

1. **Identity as a full clause** — not "a brand consultancy" (a label), but "That's what we fix at Outlook Orange:" followed by the actual steps
2. **A labelled mechanism** — Diagnostic / Workshop / Blueprint / Build — so the reader knows exactly what happens
3. **An explicit outcome** — what the prospect gets at the end, stated in one sentence
4. **A real CTA with a low-friction alternative** — a call as the main ask, the free brand audit as a P.S. fallback

## What changed in each email

| Email | V3 | V4 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** | Pain only, no mechanism, soft CTA ("Recognise any of that?") | Pain + mechanism + outcome + real CTA + brand audit P.S. | The biggest fix. Now the reader knows what OO does and what they're being asked. |
| **E2** | NUVO proof, soft CTA ("Worth a conversation?") | Same NUVO proof, CTA swapped to brand audit offer | De-escalation. E1 offered a call; E2 drops to the audit — lower friction after silence. |
| **E3** | AI confession, no CTA ("I'd genuinely like to hear about it") | Same confession, conditional call CTA added | "I'd like to hear about it" asks for nothing. Now there's an actual ask. |
| **E4** | Three triggers, passive close ("thanks for your time") | Same triggers, named duration + detached value + "won't follow up" P.S. | Gives the prospect a reason to say yes even if undecided. Clean final close. |

## The CTA ladder

- **E1:** Call + brand audit P.S. (full offer)
- **E2:** Brand audit only (de-escalation after silence)
- **E3:** Conditional call (re-escalation for those engaged by the AI angle)
- **E4:** Time-bound call + detached value + hard close

This mirrors the Alumni sequence exactly, which is now live and performing.

## What stayed from V3

- The anti-AI writing patterns: varied sentence length, no colon-tricolons, concrete language, real opinions
- NUVO Rubber as the proof point
- The AI-confession differentiator in E3
- The three triggers in E4 (not four — the AI pattern)
- Every P.S. doing a different job
