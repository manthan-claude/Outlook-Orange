## Email 1 — Day 0

**Subject:** A thought on {{companyName}}

---

Hi {{firstName}},

We do brand and positioning work for manufacturers, so I'll get straight to it.

Most of the companies we meet have outgrown the way they look to the market. The business has moved on. The brand hasn't quite caught up. Where you feel it is in the quotes you lose on price, and in buyers who treat you as interchangeable with the supplier down the road.

That's what we fix at Outlook Orange:

Diagnostic — where the brand stands
Workshop — leadership agrees what you stand for
Blueprint — guidelines everything else follows
Build — website, marketing, the lot

Worth a short conversation to see if this is relevant for {{companyName}}?

Bonita

P.S. If a call feels premature, I've put together a short brand read on {{companyName}} — a few spots where what you're saying as a business may not match how the market reads you. Happy to send over the one-pager.

|---|---|---|
| E1 | Call + personalized audit P.S. | Full offer — here's what we do, here's the ask; audit one-pager as soft entry |
| E2 | Personalized audit on {{companyName}} | De-escalation — "I've already looked at your brand, want to see what I found?" |
| E3 | One-pager + call option | Re-escalation — the AI angle earns the right to offer both paths |

# KEY DIFFERENCES: EMAIL vs LINKEDIN

| | Email | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|
| **Opener** | Direct, gets to it | Slightly warmer, "I'll keep this short" |
| **Length** | Longer, includes P.S. lines | Shorter, no P.S. on early messages |
| **Proof (E2/LI2)** | Full NUVO narrative with opinion | Condensed NUVO, bridges to "most clients arrive the same way" |
| **E3/LI3** | AI differentiator, identical | AI differentiator, "messages" instead of "emails" |
| **Closer** | Half hour + detached value + P.S. close | Dual option (audit or call) in one paragraph |
| **Sign-off** | "Bonita" on every email | "Bonita" on every message |
